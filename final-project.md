# Final Project

Submit the following:

1. **Previous lab checklist:** Confirm that deliverables for each lab were submitted via Canvas, Box, and/or GitHub.
2. **One-page overview:**

   - Project description
   - Motivation: What art historical, humanities, or aesthetic research questions are you posing/exploring?
   - AI disclosure: What tools/systems did you use? What was your relationship to them, and how was agency shared between you and the AI?

3. **Final project files:** Submit all assets, code, and project files via Canvas, Box, and/or GitHub.
4. **System diagram:** Show how your project’s components connect. This can take the form of a cybernetic diagram, flowchart, concept map, or system architecture diagram. Draw it by hand and scan it, or use Excalidraw.
5. **Video walkthrough:** Submit a screen recording demonstrating and explaining your project. Aim for 3–5 minutes.
